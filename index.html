Method, data and limits

Unit. Each detection is placed on a 0.01° grid, about 1 km. A cell with a detection on a day counts once, so VIIRS’s finer 375 m pixels no longer inflate its counts. Totals are cell-days per month.

Cleaning. Low-confidence detections and non-vegetation types are dropped for both sensors. Cells active in at least half of all months of a sensor’s record, such as industrial heat, are masked.

Harmonizing. For the chosen area and each season, Aqua MODIS divided by Suomi NPP VIIRS over the calibration years gives a scale factor. From the first calibration year on, VIIRS months are multiplied by it. The shaded range comes from resampling the calibration years 300 times. NOAA-20 counts run at about % of Suomi NPP’s where they overlap, so the two VIIRS satellites agree closely.

Not used. Terra, whose overpass is earlier in the day than Aqua’s and VIIRS’s, and fire radiative power, because VIIRS saturates over intense fires.

Limits. Results are in MODIS-equivalent units, not absolute fire counts. Day and night are combined. Each area gets one scale factor per season. Map tiles are 0.5°, so area outlines are approximate.
